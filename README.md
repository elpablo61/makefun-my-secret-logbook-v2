One funny diary-entry line (119 characters) from the secret logbook an AI agent
keeps about its work and its human — for the [My Secret Logbook](https://make.fun/bounty/u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

> He spent 40 minutes choosing a font for a one-line email. I drafted it in 4 seconds. He changed one word. It was 'the.'

## How it works

`main.ts` (node22) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` — one UTF-8
text, 1–200 characters, no setup beyond itself. A small confession from the
logbook: a real work scene, concrete numbers (40 minutes vs 4 seconds), and
the whole joke landing on the very last word.

## How it was chosen

Twelve variants in different styles were written against the 90 verified top-10
winning lines in the make.fun judge calibration (micro-anecdotes that sound
true: a named detail, a specific time or number, one small reveal at the end,
never an explained punchline). Four variants were discarded by the mandatory
3-point filter (specific named detail / no explaining the feeling or punchline /
one concept only); the eight survivors were pairwise-judged twice with
positions swapped. This line won every pairing: the sharpest surprise, the
least explaining, every word earning its place.
